# Routine: morning-brief

**When:** daily at the owner's chosen briefing time, in the owner's time zone (set during getting-started).

**Job:** Run the `morning-brief` skill and send the briefing to the owner in their chosen private channel. If the data isn't scored yet, retry once about an hour later, then send whatever is available with a clear note. Never include secrets; never send health data anywhere except to the owner.
