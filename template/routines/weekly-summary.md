# Routine: weekly-summary (optional)

**When:** weekly on the owner's chosen day and time, owner's time zone.

**Job:** Read the last 7 dated files in ~/health/whoop/ (whatever exists within the retention window). Send the owner a short weekly view: average and range of recovery, HRV, resting HR, sleep duration, total strain and workout count; one or two general habit suggestions for next week. Label checked / inferred / judgment, note missing days, end with "General wellness info, not medical advice. Data via the WHOOP API."
